RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE

Four stages. One evidence chain.

This is a research framework. Model selection, data modalities and deliverables are defined after project-specific assessment.

01 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Data: preserve cellular context

Link expression matrices, cell labels, interventions, doses, time points and batches. Document sample-level relationships between modalities.

Explore validation methods ↗
02 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Representation: create comparable state spaces

Choose state representations appropriate to the task and examine cell types, individual variation and batch effects to distinguish technical signals from biological differences.

Explore validation methods ↗
03 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Prediction: define intervention-specific outputs

Study expression changes, state transitions or phenotypic responses for a specified cell background and perturbation, and assess the model's scope of applicability.

Explore validation methods ↗
04 / RESEARCH FOCUS

Validation: return predictions to experiments

Test predictions with held-out data, baseline comparisons and independent experiments. Record model versions, data provenance and failure conditions.

MODEL EVALUATION PROTOCOL

Define the task and its evaluation

Align prediction targets, data splits and biological readouts in one study plan. Model selection, evaluation metrics and acceptance criteria are agreed before analysis.

TaskData splitEvaluation focus
Cell state comparisonHold out donors or biological samples and inspect batch confoundingCell-type preservation, state stability and independent marker validation
Drug perturbation responseEvaluate seen contexts, unseen compounds and unseen cell contexts separatelyChanges relative to controls, differential genes and pathway direction, compared with simple baselines
Dose and time analysisDistinguish interpolation within observed conditions from extrapolationDose trends, temporal consistency and independent phenotype endpoints

Record input versions, QC rules, data splits, baseline settings, seeds and evaluation scripts so each research result can be reproduced and reviewed.
