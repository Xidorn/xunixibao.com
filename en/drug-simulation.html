01 / DRUG RESPONSE SIMULATION

Research question

Does a candidate compound produce consistent responses across cellular backgrounds? How do responses change with dose and exposure time?

02 / DRUG RESPONSE SIMULATION

Input data

Controlled drug perturbation data, compound identifiers, cellular background, doses, exposure times and task-relevant experimental endpoints.

03 / DRUG RESPONSE SIMULATION

Expected outputs

Expression or phenotype responses, candidate rankings and differential pathway signals. Transcriptomic changes are not direct substitutes for viability or clinical efficacy.

04 / DRUG RESPONSE SIMULATION

Validation approach

Validate with independent replicates and external samples. Compare dose curves and key phenotypes to distinguish general stress from compound-specific effects.

Define the scope using the available data and validation conditions. Generalization to unseen cell types, perturbations or experimental systems requires separate evaluation.
